Your pathway

Grow at your own pace

Move up a level whenever your coach and you agree you are ready.

🌱

Beginner

New to the sport

Learn the rules, basic skills and safe movement.

  • 2 sessions a week
  • Small groups
⚡

Intermediate

1 to 2 years in

Build consistency, fitness and game understanding.

  • 3 sessions a week
  • Friendly matches
🔥

Advanced

Match ready

Sharpen technique, tactics and strength for competition.

  • 4 sessions a week
  • League fixtures

  • 5 sessions a week
  • Video analysis

Your first day

What to expect at a trial

A trial session is free and there is no pressure to join. You will train with a coach, meet your group and see the facilities.

  • ✓ Arrive 15 minutes early to meet your coach
  • ✓ Short warm-up and skills check
  • ✓ A guided session with your batch
  • ✓ A quick chat about goals and next steps
