Quick answers

Before you write to us

How fast will you reply?

We answer every message within one working day, usually sooner.

Can I visit without booking?

Yes. Walk in during opening hours and ask for a coach.

Is there a trial session?

Yes. The first session is free so you can try before you join.

Can I book for my child?

Yes. A parent or guardian signs a short consent form at the first session.

Do you run holiday camps?

We run camps in school breaks. Ask us for the next dates.

Where do I find my batch timings?

Your coach shares batch timings when you enrol, and they are on the Sports Programs page.

Ready to say hello?

Call, message or just walk in. We would love to meet you.
